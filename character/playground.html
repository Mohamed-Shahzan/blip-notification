Sprout is the chosen character; the other concepts are kept below as an archive. Pick a state, tune the sliders, then copy the settings.

State colours

Contrast of each colour against the background. Shapes need at least 3:1 to stand out (WCAG 1.4.11); red numbers fall short. "Backing" is the dark backing plate.

Options

Click a character to poke it. Quick clicks on Sprout: 3 and it says what it is doing, 6 or more and it gets fed up (tired eyes, antenna flopped over) until you stop. The archived concepts flip.
