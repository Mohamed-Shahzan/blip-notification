3. Done colour against needs you and error

Each candidate as needs you, done, error at 32 px. Use "View as" to see them the way people with colour blindness do.

Numbers are how far apart two colours look (CIE76 colour difference after the colour-blind simulation). A rough rule of thumb, not a standard: under about 15 reads as the same colour at this size, over 30 is clearly different. Orange marks under 25.
